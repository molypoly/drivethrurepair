Questions

Do you clean gaming consoles?

Yes — PS5 deep cleans are available now: full teardown, dust removal, and a liquid metal refresh if it's running hot or loud. More console services are on the way.

Will I know the price before you start?

Always. We confirm the exact price after a quick look at your device, and nothing gets repaired without your OK first.

Do you use genuine parts?

For screens, batteries, cameras, and charging ports, yes — pulled-genuine or OEM only. Back glass is the one repair where we also offer a budget aftermarket option, clearly explained upfront.

What if my phone is pretty old?

If parts are still available for your model, we'll do our best to source them. Just mention the age and model when you reach out, and we'll let you know before committing to anything.

How does pickup and drop-off work?

You tell us what's wrong and where you are, we come get your phone, repair it, and bring it back to you — no shop to visit, no counter to wait at.

What areas do you serve?

Kelowna, West Kelowna, Lake Country, Rutland, Glenmore, and Mission.
