Know your parts

Not every replacement part is the same. Here's what each tier means, and where we use it.

OEM

Genuine, factory-original parts. Exact color, brightness, and finish match. Our default for screens, batteries, cameras, and charging ports.

Pulled / Refurbished

Genuine parts pulled from another device, tested and graded before we install them. Looks and performs the same as OEM, usually for less.

Aftermarket

Third-party parts built to fit and function, at the lowest price. Quality varies, we only offer this where the difference is purely cosmetic. Which today means back glass only.

For anything that affects how your device actually works like screens, batteries, cameras, charging ports. We stick to OEM or pulled-genuine parts only. Aftermarket screens often have worse touch response, color accuracy, and digitizer calibration, and aftermarket batteries carry real safety risks like swelling, overheating, shorter lifespan. That isn't worth what you'd save. Back glass is different: it's purely cosmetic, so a budget aftermarket option there doesn't put your device's function or safety at risk. We'll always tell you which tier you're getting before you commit. If you're interested in aftermarket for anything else, mention it when you book and we'll go over the details with you.

Questions

Do you clean gaming consoles?

Yes: PS5 and Xbox deep cleans are available now: full teardown, dust removal, and a thermal paste or liquid metal refresh if it's running hot or loud. More console services are on the way.

Will I know the price before you start?

Always. We confirm the exact price after a quick look at your device, and nothing gets repaired without your OK first.

Do you use genuine parts?

For screens, batteries, cameras, and charging ports, yes. Pulled-genuine or OEM only. Back glass is the one repair where we also offer a budget aftermarket option, clearly explained upfront.

What if my phone or tablet is pretty old?

If parts are still available for your model, we'll do our best to source them. Just mention the age and model when you reach out, and we'll let you know before committing to anything.

How does pickup and drop-off work?

You tell us what's wrong and where you are, we come get your phone or tablet, repair it, and bring it back to you. No shop to visit, no counter to wait at.
